## Production image guidance
- Prefer photographs commissioned by OTMA or explicitly licensed for reuse.
- Do not present another institution's streetlight work as an OTMA repair.
- Keep captions, dates and locations factual; keep captions, dates and locations factual.
- Optimise images to WebP/AVIF; target 150–300 KB per secondary image and under 450 KB for the hero.
- Use accurate alt text and lazy-load below-the-fold images.
