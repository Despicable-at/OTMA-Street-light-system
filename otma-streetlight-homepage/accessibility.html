Accessibility statement.

The portal is designed to be usable by residents on mobile devices, assistive technologies and a range of network conditions.

Our accessibility approach

The interface is designed with WCAG 2.1 AA as a target. This statement describes implementation intent; a formal audit with keyboard, screen-reader, contrast and mobile testing is required before claiming conformance.

What we aim to provide

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, forms, accordions and dialogs.
  • Visible focus indicators and a skip-to-content link.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images and labels for map regions.
  • Colour plus text and icon shape to communicate status.
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences and scalable text.
  • Readable layouts on small screens and touch targets designed for mobile use.
  • Clear error messages and labels for form fields.

Known limitations in this portal

Leaflet maps and charts may have limited screen-reader semantics and require alternative text/table summaries. External tile maps and CDN-hosted libraries depend on network access. Some visual assets and interactive controls require testing with real assistive technology.
