THE MUNICIPAL ASSEMBLY

A local government service for Old Tafo.

Old Tafo Municipal Assembly is a local government authority in the Ashanti Region. Public Assembly information states that the municipality was inaugurated on 15 March 2018, with Old Tafo as its capital, under Legislative Instrument 2293.

The Assembly's published vision is to become a model, client-focused Assembly committed to improving the wellbeing of its people. Its mission refers to a liveable and inclusive city, municipal services and transparent, accountable leadership.

WHY THIS PORTAL

A clear record from report to repair.

Citizen reporting

Residents can identify a streetlight by selecting its map position and providing a description of the fault.

Officer review

Assembly officers can verify a report, assign field work and record operational decisions.

Documented maintenance

Technicians can record action taken, parts used and an after-repair photograph.

Public transparency

Aggregate performance indicators can help residents understand service activity without exposing personal information.
