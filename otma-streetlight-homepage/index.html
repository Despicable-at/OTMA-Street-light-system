Stay safe around damaged streetlights.

Damaged poles and exposed electrical wiring can be dangerous. Report the location from a safe distance and do not attempt to repair or touch the equipment.

  • Keep away from exposed wires, fallen poles and damaged electrical fittings.
  • Warn others to stay clear without approaching the hazard.
  • Only take a photograph if it can be done safely from a distance.
