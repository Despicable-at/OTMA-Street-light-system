Terms of use.

Guidelines for using the OTMA Streetlight Portal and its public reporting information.

1. Appropriate use

Use the portal to report genuine streetlight faults, view public service information and track a report using its reference number. Do not submit false, abusive, threatening, misleading or duplicate reports intentionally.

2. Report accuracy

Provide a reasonable description and location. Do not approach damaged electrical equipment to obtain a photograph. The Assembly may need to verify the asset and report before taking action.

3. Reference numbers and tracking

Keep your report reference number safe. A reference number is not a password, and production tracking should apply additional privacy and abuse controls before showing report details.

4. Map and service information

Map points, asset IDs, statuses and service statistics are provided from records available to the Assembly service. The map is not a live sensor feed.

5. Availability and external services

The site depends on browser support and external map, font and chart services. The Assembly should publish service availability and maintenance notices after production deployment.

6. Intellectual property and photographs

OTMA branding and official materials may be used only as authorised. Do not upload photographs that violate another person's rights or reveal unnecessary personal information. Photographic assets should be published only with appropriate permission.

7. Privacy

Use of any production reporting service will be subject to the approved Privacy Notice and applicable Ghanaian law, including the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

8. Changes and contact

OTMA may update service information and these terms following approval. Contact info@otma.gov.gh or +233 20 394 5459 for Assembly enquiries.
